# 18 常见失败与修复

## 灯体发光后边缘消失
加：clear fixture contours even when illuminated, retain diffuser edge thickness, controlled bloom, highlight detail preserved.

## 黑色灯体一片死黑
加：subtle edge highlights, visible matte surface texture, shadow detail retained.

## 金色像黄色塑料
加：real metallic brass/gold finish, directional specular highlights, realistic metal roughness.

## 玻璃球像白塑料球
加：transparent glass with realistic refraction, visible glass thickness, controlled internal reflection.

## 墙灯不水平
加：strictly level installation, bottom edge parallel to the floor, straight geometric alignment, corrected architectural perspective.

## 吸顶灯漂浮
加：chassis fully flush with ceiling, realistic ceiling contact shadow, no gap.

## AI多加灯臂
加：exact arm count, exact topology, no extra parts, no duplicated arms.

## 场景太廉价
减少装饰物，增加：restrained premium styling, coherent materials, architectural cleanliness, commercial interior design.

## 放大后背景融化
加：deep detail retention throughout the scene, realistic furniture edges, resolved fabric texture, no painterly background interpolation.
