# 16 Prompt Compiler — 提示词编译器

最终提示词必须形成完整摄影描述，而不是关键词垃圾堆。

## Step 1 — Product Lock
例：
"Preserve the exact reference ceiling light: five black aluminum petal arms, white central chassis, identical petal geometry, exact proportions, exact light-emitting acrylic surfaces, no redesign."

## Step 2 — Installation
例：
"Mounted flush and level at the exact center of a flat white ceiling, chassis fully contacting the ceiling, no gaps or floating parts."

## Step 3 — Scene
例：
"Bright modern living room with light beige sofa, natural stone coffee table, pale oak accents, large window and restrained decor."

## Step 4 — Scale
例：
"The fixture has realistic scale relative to the room and occupies a clear but not oversized proportion of the frame."

## Step 5 — Light State
例：
"Lamp switched on at 3000K warm white, diffuser remains visible, controlled glow, no clipped highlights."

## Step 6 — Lighting Design
例：
"Soft daylight from the side combined with controlled interior fill, realistic contact shadows and ambient reflections."

## Step 7 — Camera
例：
"High-end architectural commercial photography, medium-format look, corrected verticals, moderate wide-angle perspective, deep enough depth of field to retain product and room detail."

## Step 8 — Materials
描述每种核心材质，而不是只写 realistic.

## Step 9 — Resolution
加入结构性高清词：edge integrity, micro-textures, clean contours, fine seams, low noise.

## Step 10 — Negative
只加入与任务相关且价值高的负面词。

## 最终尾词（通用）
"premium commercial lighting photography, exact product identity preservation, highly resolved micro-details, crisp geometric edges, realistic material roughness, controlled highlights, smooth tonal roll-off, low-noise shadows, high dynamic range, realistic optical rendering, RAW-quality tonal detail, suitable for high-resolution commercial use"
